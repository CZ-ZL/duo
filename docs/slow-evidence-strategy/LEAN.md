## Review-driven completion pass (0.6.0)

- Settled-only cross-run admission → existing-ledger exposure including full
  unfinished allowances, plus a short exclusive claim/initialization lock.
  Why: paused work cannot lose its promised budget to a new run. No new ledger
  or scheduler. Regression: paused/live allowance, unknown cost, parallel native
  processes, active cumulative cap inherited by an otherwise uncapped contract.
- Schema then origin / claim then limits → atomic schema+origin and immutable
  limits before publishing claim under the admission lock. Read-only preflight
  reports busy during initialization. Independent two-process probes exposed
  and then verified both initialization interleavings.
- Choose preferred then validate coverage → visit Comparator order, accept the
  first policy-admissible candidate. A held first choice no longer suppresses
  a valid runner-up. Gate proposal and one actual selected candidate are distinct;
  effective decisions feed Journal/report/history consistently.
- Discovery ↔ evidence-strategy imports → shared pure evidence-source metadata
  helpers. Original public exports retained; no extra service registration.
- Dense one-line product modules → consistent Prettier formatting. This improves
  reviewability and increases physical line count; it is not claimed as LOC
  reduction. Runtime behavior is protected by the full native/package gate.
- Generic strategy hook payloads → explicit typed observations, aggregates,
  acquisition options and decisions. Positive and rejected-input controls compile
  against public exports. Runtime validation remains intact.
- Separate prose capability copies → existing generated catalog remains the
  authority; English/Chinese READMEs link to it. Historical receipts are not edited.
